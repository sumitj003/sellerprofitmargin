About SellerProfitMargin

Built by sellers, for sellers. We believe every ecommerce seller deserves to know their real numbers before listing a single product.

Why we built this

Too many sellers list products on Etsy, Shopify, and Amazon without knowing their actual profit margin. They see revenue growing and assume they're making money — until they check their bank account.

Platform fees, shipping costs, advertising spend, and product costs add up fast. A $30 product selling for $50 can easily return just $8 in real profit after Etsy's cut, shipping, and ads.

SellerProfitMargin was built to give sellers clarity in seconds — free, no signup, no hidden fees.
