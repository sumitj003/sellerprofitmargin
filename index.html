Platform Fee Guides — Calculate Profit on Any Marketplace

Understanding exact fees is the difference between a profitable product and an invisible loss. Here's what each platform charges:

🛍️ Etsy Seller Fees 2025

6.5% + $0.20 + ~3.25%

Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee on the full sale price including shipping, a $0.20 listing fee per item, and ~3% + $0.25 payment processing. Offsite Ads adds 12–15% on qualifying sales. Use the Etsy tab above to calculate your exact margin.

Full Etsy fee guide →

📦 Amazon FBA Fees Explained

15% referral + $3–$8 FBA

Amazon charges a ~15% referral fee on most categories plus FBA fulfillment fees of $3.22–$8+ per unit based on size and weight. Add monthly storage fees and PPC advertising costs for accurate margin calculation.

Full Amazon FBA fee guide →

🔵 Shopify Profit Margin Formula

2.9% + $0.30 per order

Shopify Payments charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction on Basic plan. No per-sale marketplace commission. Factor in your $39/month subscription divided by order volume plus marketing costs for true net margin.

Full Shopify margin guide →

🟡 Walmart Marketplace Seller Fees

6–15% referral, no monthly fee

Walmart charges a 6–15% referral fee (averaging ~8%) with no monthly subscription unlike Amazon. No listing fees. Optional WFS (Walmart Fulfillment Services) fees apply if you use their warehousing.

🔴 eBay Final Value Fee Calculator

12–15% + fixed per-order fee

eBay charges a 12–15% final value fee depending on your category and store plan, plus a small fixed per-order fee. Basic sellers pay the higher rate. An eBay Store subscription reduces fees for high-volume sellers.

🎵 Free TikTok Shop Profit Margin Calculator

~6% referral + 2% processing

TikTok Shop charges approximately 6% referral fee on most categories plus ~2% payment processing. Creator affiliate commissions of 5–20% are additional costs if you use their affiliate marketing program.

Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate Etsy profit margin?
Etsy profit margin = (Selling Price − Product Cost − Shipping − All Etsy Fees) ÷ Selling Price × 100. Etsy fees include 6.5% transaction fee, $0.20 listing fee per item, and ~3% + $0.25 payment processing. A healthy Etsy margin is 25–40%.
What is a good profit margin for Amazon FBA?
Most successful Amazon FBA sellers target 25–35% net margin. Amazon charges ~15% referral fee plus FBA fulfillment fees. Aim for minimum 20% margin to cover returns, storage fees, and PPC advertising costs.
How do I calculate break-even price?
Break-even price is the minimum price you must charge to cover all costs — product, shipping, fees, ads — with zero profit. Our calculator shows this automatically. Price below break-even and you lose money on every sale.
How many units do I need to sell to break even?
Break-even units = Monthly Fixed Costs ÷ Net Profit Per Unit. If your fixed costs are $500/month and profit per unit is $12.50, you need 40 units per month to break even. Use the Break-Even Volume tool above to calculate your exact number automatically.
Is this free TikTok Shop profit margin calculator really free?
Yes — completely free, no signup, no email required. Supports all 6 platforms: Etsy, Shopify, Amazon FBA, Walmart, eBay, and TikTok Shop. TikTok Shop fee is pre-loaded at 6% referral fee plus approximately 2% payment processing.
Can I compare two pricing scenarios?
Yes — use the Scenario Compare tool in the Advanced Tools section. Load your current calculation, modify any value in Scenario B (different price, platform, shipping cost), and click Compare. It shows side-by-side profit, margin, and break-even with a difference callout.
How do Walmart Marketplace seller fees work?
Walmart Marketplace charges a referral fee of 6–15% depending on product category, averaging approximately 8%. Unlike Amazon, there is no monthly subscription fee. Optional Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) fees apply only if you use their warehousing.
