Frequently asked questions
How is profit margin calculated?
Profit margin = (Net Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100. Net profit is your selling price minus product cost, shipping, platform fee, and ad spend. A healthy margin for ecommerce is typically 20–40%.
What is the Etsy seller fee in 2025?
Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee on the full sale price including shipping, a $0.20 listing fee per item, and payment processing of ~3% + $0.25 per transaction. If Etsy promotes your listing externally via Offsite Ads, an additional 12–15% fee applies to those sales. Our calculator includes all these when you use the Etsy tab and toggle Offsite Ads on if applicable.
What is a good profit margin for Amazon FBA?
Most successful Amazon sellers target 25–35% net margin. Amazon charges ~15% referral fee plus FBA fulfillment fees. Aim for minimum 20% margin to cover returns, storage fees, and PPC costs.
How do I calculate break-even price?
Break-even is the minimum price you need to charge to cover all costs — product, shipping, fees, ads — with zero profit. Our calculator shows this automatically. Price below this and you're losing money on every sale.
Does this work for digital products?
Yes — set shipping and product cost to $0 for digital items. Your only costs are the platform fee and any ad spend. Digital products typically have the highest margins on Etsy, often 60–90%.
Can I use this for Shopify dropshipping?
Absolutely. Select the Shopify tab (pre-loaded at 2.9%), enter your supplier price as product cost, add shipping, and include your ad spend. The $39/month Shopify subscription is a fixed cost — divide it by your monthly order volume and add it to the product cost field for a fully accurate margin.
What are TikTok Shop fees?
TikTok Shop currently charges around 6% referral fee on most product categories, plus payment processing of approximately 2%. If you use their affiliate creator program, creators typically earn 5–20% commission which is an additional cost on affiliate-attributed sales.
How does the currency switcher work?
The currency selector in the top right corner lets you switch between USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD and AUD. All results — profit, break-even, and insight figures — convert using current approximate exchange rates. Enter your numbers in your selling currency and results display accordingly.
