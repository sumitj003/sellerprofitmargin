Frequently asked questions
How is profit margin calculated?
Profit margin = (Net Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100. Net profit is your selling price minus all costs: product cost, shipping, platform fee, and ad spend. A healthy margin for ecommerce is typically 20–40%.
What is the Etsy seller fee in 2025?
Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee on the full sale price including shipping, a $0.20 listing fee per item, and payment processing of ~3% + $0.25 per transaction. If Etsy promotes your listing externally via Offsite Ads, an additional 12–15% fee applies. Our calculator includes all of these.
What is a good profit margin for Amazon FBA?
Most successful Amazon sellers target 25–35% net margin. Amazon charges ~15% referral fee plus FBA fulfillment fees. Aim for minimum 20% margin to cover returns, storage fees, and PPC costs.
What are TikTok Shop fees?
TikTok Shop charges around 6% referral fee on most product categories plus payment processing of ~2%. If you use their affiliate creator program, additional creator commission applies on affiliate-attributed sales.
How do Walmart Marketplace fees work?
Walmart Marketplace charges a referral fee of 6–15% depending on product category (averaging ~8%), with no monthly subscription fee. Optional Walmart Fulfillment Services fees apply if you use their warehousing.
Can I use this for Shopify dropshipping?
Yes. Select the Shopify tab (2.9% payment processing), enter your supplier price as product cost, add shipping, and include your ad spend. The $39/month Shopify subscription — divide by monthly order volume and add to product cost for a fully accurate margin.
How does the currency switcher work?
The currency dropdown in the top right corner lets you switch between USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD and AUD. All results convert using current approximate exchange rates.
