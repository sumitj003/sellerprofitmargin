Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains what SellerProfitMargin.com collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to everything at www.sellerprofitmargin.com.

The short version: the calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device and we never see them. What we do collect is ordinary website analytics and advertising cookies, described below.

What the calculators do with your numbers

Every calculator on this site is JavaScript running inside your own browser. Your prices, costs and margins are not sent to any server, not stored, and not logged. Close the tab and they are gone. There is no account, no signup and no email required to use anything here.

Information we collect

Information you give us

If you email us through the contact page, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics record standard technical data when you visit:

  • IP address, in a shortened or anonymised form
  • Browser type, operating system and device type
  • Pages viewed, time on page, and the page you arrived from
  • Approximate location at country or city level

This is aggregate data. We use it to see which pages are useful and which are not. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. This site uses them in two ways: for basic analytics, and for advertising.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The calculators will continue to work normally if you do.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit this site. These companies may use information about your visits to this and other websites in order to show ads for goods and services that may interest you.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads on this site and may likewise use cookies.

How to opt out

  • Turn off personalised advertising from Google at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies collectively at aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.

We have no control over the cookies used by third-party advertisers and cannot remove them on your behalf. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes the data described above. You can prevent it entirely using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to ecommerce tools and services. If you click one and sign up, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never change which tools we recommend or what our calculators tell you. Where a link is an affiliate link, it is marked as such.

If you are in the EU or UK

Under GDPR our legal basis for processing analytics and advertising data is your consent, given through the cookie notice, and our legitimate interest in running and improving the site. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, email us using the contact page.

If you are in California

Under the CCPA and CPRA you may request disclosure of the personal information collected about you, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, use the contact page.

Children

This site is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept in aggregate form for up to 26 months. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because the calculators never transmit your figures, there is no financial data on our side to secure.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change, and revise the date at the top. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the contact page.
