Why TikTok Shop margins disappear

TikTok Shop looks cheaper than Amazon on paper. The platform cut is smaller. But three things stack on top of it that sellers rarely price for, and together they are usually what turns a profitable-looking product into a break-even one.

Creator commission is a real cost, not marketing

Affiliate commission is the main engine of TikTok Shop. Most sellers offer 10 to 20 percent to get creators to pick up a product. That comes straight off the top of every sale a creator drives, and it is easy to forget when you are looking at a product's cost sheet. Set it in the calculator above and watch what a 20 percent rate does to a 30 percent margin.

Free shipping is not free

TikTok buyers expect free shipping and conversion drops sharply without it. But the postage still gets paid, out of your margin, while platform fees are still charged on the full order value. A $4.80 shipping cost on a $29.99 product is 16 percent of the price gone before anything else.

Discounts and flash sales

Campaign discounts, coupon codes and live-stream flash pricing are usually funded by the seller, not the platform. Run a 15 percent flash sale on a product with a 30 percent margin and half the profit goes with it.

What to aim for

Price for more margin than you would on other marketplaces. Between fees, creator commission and the discounting the platform encourages, sellers who build in 25 percent often finish near zero. Most people who last on TikTok Shop are building 40 to 50 percent into the price before creator commission, which leaves them room to fund a promotion without going backwards.

The break-even number above is the one to write down. It is the price below which a flash sale costs you money rather than making it.

Questions people ask

How much does TikTok Shop take per sale?

A referral fee plus a transaction fee on each order. Rates vary by category and country and TikTok has changed them several times since launch, so check the current rate for your category in Seller Center. Creator commission, if you use affiliates, sits on top and is set by you.

Does TikTok Shop charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Fees are worked out on the order total including any shipping the buyer paid. Offering free shipping does not reduce the fee, it just moves the postage cost onto you.

What margin should I aim for on TikTok Shop?

Higher than elsewhere. Build in 40 to 50 percent before creator commission. Sellers who price for 25 percent tend to end up at zero once commission and discounts land.

How much affiliate commission should I offer creators?

Usually 10 to 20 percent. Below 10 percent few creators pick the product up. Above 20 percent only works on a very wide margin. Set the rate above and see what it leaves before you commit to it.
